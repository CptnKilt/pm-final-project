# Problem Hook & Value Proposition, Module 1

- **Scenario:** StreamLine Spotlight (B2C)

## Strategic crisis
The subscriber sees Streamline as the place that has everything, but not the place to have a great cinema experience. Subscriber engagement will continue to flatten or decline as the need for a more curated experinence is desired. The current problems compound while appearing manageable quarter to quarter, making recovery more expensive later.

## Moment of misery
Currently the user is having to curate their viewing options themselves within a overwhelming video libary.

## Problem hook
StreamLine’s biggest library is becoming its biggest liability: overwhelmed viewers are forced to curate it themselves, driving disengagement and churn while competitors win their trust.

## Value proposition
For high-value viewers who want great cinema without endless searching, we will deliver trusted, human-curated discovery because rising choice fatigue and churn is turning our most engaged audiences into the competitors' most loyal customers.
