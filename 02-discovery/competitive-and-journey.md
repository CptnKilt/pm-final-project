# StreamLine Spotlight — Future State Journey

**Persona:** Marcus, 41 — a taste-driven film enthusiast who still actively uses the platform but is increasingly unconvinced it understands him.

**Goal:** He wants to be understood as an individual with specific taste, not sorted into a genre bucket and fed more of the same.

**Friction:** *"The 'Because you watched' row is a joke, it recommended three more action sequels because I watched one. I'm not a genre, I'm a person. I miss when a human curator would surface a hidden gem."* (Corroborated by BUG-1091 — near-duplicate, low-diversity recommendations.)

---

## The 4 Stages

### 1. Discovery Without a Pitch

- **User Action:** Opens the app out of habit, notices a named curator's collection instead of "Because you watched."
- **Internal State:** Guarded — expects to be sorted into a genre again.
- **Pain Point Addressed:** Being read as a genre, not a person.
- **Action → Benefit:** Sees a human name, not a formula → guard drops enough to look twice.

### 2. The Editorial Test

- **User Action:** Opens the collection, reads the curator's point of view on why these titles belong together.
- **Internal State:** Curious, comparing this voice against the "hidden gem" curator he misses.
- **Pain Point Addressed:** No taste, no point of view, just volume.
- **Action → Benefit:** Reads an actual rationale → recognizes discernment, not an algorithm's guess.

### 3. The Trusted Pick

- **User Action:** Presses play on a title he would never have searched for himself, on the curator's word.
- **Internal State:** Slightly surprised — this is the "hidden gem" feeling he described missing.
- **Pain Point Addressed:** The exact moment "Because you watched" broke trust.
- **Action → Benefit:** Watches something unexpected and good → in-app discovery earns credibility again.

### 4. Discovery Reclaimed

- **User Action:** Follows the curator, returns to Spotlight first — before checking outside sources.
- **Internal State:** Re-anchored — the app is a discovery authority again, not just a delivery pipe.
- **Pain Point Addressed:** Routing the real decision outside the platform.
- **Action → Benefit:** Makes Spotlight the first stop → the manual workaround becomes unnecessary.

---

## Three Competitive Advantages Over Marcus's Workaround

1. **Closes the feedback loop.** His external curation never reaches the recommendation engine today. Spotlight brings taste signal back inside the product.
2. **Collapses two steps into one.** He currently decides elsewhere, then searches by exact title. Spotlight lets discovery and playback happen in the same motion.
3. **Scales what a friend can't.** A named curator, unlike a single friend's taste, can maintain range and cadence — without losing the human point of view he trusts.
