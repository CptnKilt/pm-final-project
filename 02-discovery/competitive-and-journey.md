# Tom's Way Back to a Good Evening
**StreamLine Spotlight · Future-State Journey**

**Strategy:** Win back taste-driven viewers through human-led curation, without shrinking the library that funds it.

**Persona:** Tom, 52, lapsed subscriber
> "It became a warehouse. Volume went up, quality of my evenings went down." (UXR-04)

**Workaround today:** A competitor emails him two hand-picked films a week, and he watches both.

---

## The 4 Stages

### 1. The invitation
- **User action:** Opens a named curator's weekly Spotlight dispatch, sent as a win-back offer.
- **Internal state:** Skeptical. *"Is it still a warehouse?"*
- **Action → Benefit:**
  - Curator dispatch lands in his inbox → discovery still comes to him.
  - One tap opens the pick in-app → no catalog to wade through.
- **Pain point addressed:** "It became a warehouse": nobody tells him what's worth watching. *(UXR-04)*

### 2. The arrival
- **User action:** Lands in Spotlight instead of the full home grid.
- **Internal state:** Cautious relief. *"This actually feels edited."*
- **Action → Benefit:**
  - Opens straight into Spotlight → a bounded collection replaces 15,000 titles.
  - Reads the curator's note → knows why this film, not just what.
- **Pain point addressed:** Choice overload: the twenty-minute scroll that ends in nothing. *(UXR-01, UXR-12)*

### 3. The evening
- **User action:** Picks tonight's film and saves a second one for the weekend.
- **Internal state:** Trust. *"Someone with taste chose this."*
- **Action → Benefit:**
  - Chooses from a short, edited list → decides in minutes, not twenty.
  - Saves the second pick on his phone → it's waiting on the TV.
- **Pain point addressed:** Genre-clone recommendations, and saved picks lost between devices. *(BUG-1091, BUG-1058)*

### 4. The ritual
- **User action:** Follows a curator and comes back to Spotlight each week.
- **Internal state:** Belonging. *"My evenings are good again."*
- **Action → Benefit:**
  - Follows a curator who shares his taste → picks feel made for him.
  - Opens that curator's wider collection → more than two films when he wants.
- **Pain point addressed:** "Quality of my evenings went down," and no reason to come back. *(UXR-04)*

*Internal states are hypotheses to test with users, not research findings.*

---

## 3 Competitive Advantages Over the Competitor's Email

| # | Advantage | Today (manual workaround) | Spotlight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pick to play, in one place** | Read the email, open another app, find the film. | Tap the curator's pick and it plays. |
| 2 | **Depth on demand** | Two films a week, then nothing until next week. | Bounded collections, with the full library behind them when he wants more. |
| 3 | **Picks that follow him** | An email he has to dig back through to find last week's film. | Saved picks and progress on every screen. *Depends on BUG-1058 and BUG-1061 shipping first.* |

**Risk to test:** Tom's workaround already works (he watches both picks). Spotlight has to beat "good enough," not a broken process.
